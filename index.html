About the Symposium

Innovating for a Sustainable Future

Where ideas spark change and technology meets purpose

ArtiWhiz'26 is an International Student Symposium on Humanizing Technology, organized by the Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science and the Skylark Association at E.G.S. Pillay Engineering College, Nagapattinam.

Themed "AI for Sustainability", this two-day event brings together brilliant minds to explore how AI can solve the world's most pressing environmental and societal challenges.

Guided by Sustainable · Inclusive · Ethical · Responsible values, we invite students across India and beyond to compete, collaborate, and co-create.
